Kalmar Castle

Discover Kalmar Castle, Sweden

Residing in the city of Kalmar, Kalmar Castle dates back to the 12th century. Historically known as "The Key to the Kingdom", its significance in Swedish history cannot be understated.

What Can You See?

Book a tour and learn about the history connected to the castle, or admire from afar.

  • Explore the royal state apartments.
  • Walk the rampart or the grounds of the castle.
  • Roam the King's Chambers.
  • Take in the atmosphere of the Golden Hall.

Historical Significance

The placement of Kalmar Castle was strategic, lying close to the former Danish border.

The castle's history dates back to the 12th century. A defensive tower was constructed around 1180 on the site of the castle, and this defensiveness remained as a defining aspect for quite a while. It was referred to as the Key to the Kingdom due to its strategic location.

The Kalmar union was signed here in 1397. The appearance of the castle was modified in the 16th century, and it became a Renaissance building.

Plan Your Visit

Before travelling, book a tour in advance and secure a time slot. Pricing is also available on touring websites.

  • Check availability and be aware of planned closures.
  • Wear flat shoes in preparation for the uneven cobblestone floors.
  • Dress warm to combat the cold interior of the castle.
  • Check the accessibility options available on your tour if applicable.
